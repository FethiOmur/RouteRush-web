What's new in RouteRush

RouteRush first came out on the App Store on 8 September 2026. Each entry is one release, newest first, with what changed for you.

1.4.1 24 November 2026

The redesign reaches the rest of the app. The run summary now puts your three key numbers at the top, next to your reward. Territory details fit in one sheet you pull up for the runs, pace and elevation behind an area. Leaderboards, club pages, your profile and Settings are cleaner, clubs can crop a cover photo and logo before uploading, and Explore leads with the event nearest to you. Boldi's voice chat no longer ends when an answer takes a moment.

1.4.0 20 November 2026

Profile stats have cleaner charts for steps, calories and distance: tap a bar to see that day. Your territory now shows your all-time distance, on your profile and on other runners' profiles, and your streak leads your highlights. Plans were redesigned so your active plan, its weekly view and your progress read at a glance, and weekly and monthly challenges moved to the Plans tab, right under your active plan. In Club, the second tab is now Battles, for matchups against other clubs.

1.3.1 18 November 2026

The button that starts a voice conversation with Boldi now shows Boldi himself instead of a microphone: tap him in the message bar and start talking. Clubs can now be invite-only: pick Invite only in the club's settings and share the invite link. With location allowed, Boldi knows the area you are in and its weather when you chat, not only during a run. Scrolling your profile stats no longer gets stuck on the charts.

1.3.0 2 November 2026

RouteRush now fits iPhone Duo, folded and unfolded: the map, sheets, the welcome, clubs and the Live Activity adapt to both displays. Without an account you now see the real territory map, and tapping an area invites you to sign in and claim it. If location access is off, the app explains what is missing and how to switch it on.

1.2.3 30 October 2026

A new welcome starts on a live map of a real city, then pulls back to the whole Earth. Spin the globe and tap Istanbul, Paris, London, Barcelona, Rome, New York or Sydney to look around a sample week of runs and the ground they claimed. Older iPhones and Low Power Mode get the classic, lighter welcome.

1.2.2 26 October 2026

Post to Instagram Stories in one tap from run cards, the run replay video, Explore and club posts, club and event cards and achievement celebrations, or share a run replay as an Instagram Reel. The replay camera now swings cleanly behind you on sharp turnarounds. If only part of a run's GPS points uploaded on a weak connection, RouteRush now recovers the rest instead of losing the run.

1.2.1 24 October 2026

Clubs were rebuilt: search for a club and open a full club page with stats, recent activities, ranked members and events. Owners can hand over roles and ownership, and share the club with a link or a story card. A GPS badge shows when you can start, distance is more accurate, and your summary opens the moment you tap Finish, even offline. A failed post upload can now be retried, and you can report or block right from the comments.

1.2.0 17 October 2026

Boldi got one screen for chat, history and plans. When you are sick, travelling or taking a break, tell him and the plan adjusts, with the change shown before and after. Seeing who owns any territory on the map became free for everyone, and Explore gained an Events tab.

1.1.1 3 October 2026

Flyover videos now show a 3D Boldi running or riding your exact route, with a smooth camera, at a speed you pick from 1× to 4×. Boldi has a fresh look, with new moments when you start a plan, get promoted in your league and finish a run. Tapping Finish more than once while a run is saving no longer causes trouble.

1.1.0 26 September 2026

Twenty animated achievement badges arrived, twelve of them new. Territory walls now trace the border of the area you own, and the territory sheet shows the runs that built it. You can share a run straight from the map, record a flyover of your route and pick from a new filter gallery. A new monthly challenge, Run 50K, joined the weekly challenges.

1.0.1 10 September 2026

A new liquid orb shows when Boldi is thinking, and his voice waves during a run share the same look. His replies now flow onto the screen smoothly instead of jumping in. Sheets no longer turn see-through when the keyboard opens, and a false "Health access looks off" message is gone.

Get RouteRush on the App Store — free on iPhone, iOS 18 or later.
